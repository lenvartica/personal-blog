About Our Ministry

Bringing the Gospel of Jesus Christ to our community

Who We Are

Generation of Faith Ministries – Nchoroiboro is a Christ-centered church dedicated to preaching the Gospel of Jesus Christ, making disciples, and helping people grow in faith through the teaching of God's Word, prayer, worship, and community outreach.

We are led by Pastor Mary and Pastor Kibaya, whose passion is to see lives transformed by the power of Jesus Christ and to equip believers to live according to God's purpose.

Our Vision

To raise a generation of believers who are firmly rooted in faith, empowered by the Holy Spirit, and committed to serving God and their communities.

Our Mission

Preach the Gospel, teach God's Word, raise disciples, build strong families, serve with compassion, and encourage prayer and holy living.
